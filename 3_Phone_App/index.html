Talk to Momo

The head and the back button do the same things, so if one ever stops working, use the other.

  • Tapnext, pet Momo, play
  • Hold 1 second, let gochoose (on Momo's face: open the menu)
  • Double-tapback (in a game: hold to leave)
  • Keep your hand on the headrub: Momo closes its eyes and purrs
  • Hold the back button 3 secondssleep. Press it again to wake Momo.
  • Slide switchfully off (it still charges)

46 expressions

Tap one and Momo shows it.

Alarms

Tap the screen to snooze, hold to stop. Alarms ring even in quiet hours.

My reminders

Anything you must not forget, like "Book ticket" at 4:30 PM. Momo shows your words and rings until you tap it (hold = again in 5 minutes). Rings even in quiet hours.

Sound

Quiet hours

No sounds (alarms still ring). Momo is also quiet while he sleeps at night.

Your own tune

Notes like C5/8 E5/8 G5/4. Letter A-G (# for sharp), octave 1-8, /length (1, 2, 4, 8, 16). R = pause. Choose "My tune" for an alarm to wake up to it.
