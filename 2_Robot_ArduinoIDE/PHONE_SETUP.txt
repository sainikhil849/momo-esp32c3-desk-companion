MOMO 2.2 - CONNECT YOUR PHONE
=============================

Momo talks to your phone in two ways. You can use one or both.

  Momo app  (Wi-Fi, opens in Chrome, nothing to install)
     alarms, water/stretch reminders, sounds, your own tune, quiet hours,
     Momo's day (wake/sleep, play hours), games, expressions, weather city,
     Wi-Fi, paired phones, backup/restore. New in 2.2: battery level,
     Back button, Momo's look (Mini hero / Classic, head rub, low-battery
     warning), weather refresh time and source, Update now.
  Chronos   (Bluetooth, free app from the Play Store)
     phone notifications, calls, music title + control, weather, the
     alarms you set in Chronos, find my device, time.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1 - THE MOMO APP (first start, about 3 minutes)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
On the very first start Momo shows:
     Setup hotspot:  Momo-XXXX
     Password: 8 random digits   (shown only on Momo's screen)
     Then open: http://192.168.4.1

  1. Phone > Settings > Wi-Fi > join "Momo-XXXX" with that password.
     If Android says "no internet", choose "Stay connected".
  2. Chrome opens the Momo app by itself; if not, type http://192.168.4.1
  3. Press "1. Show a code on Momo". Type the 6 numbers from Momo's screen,
     give your phone a name, press "2. Pair".
  4. Tab "More" > Wi-Fi > "Find Wi-Fi networks" > Choose your home Wi-Fi >
     type its password > "Connect Momo".
  5. Momo joins your Wi-Fi and shows its address, for example
     http://192.168.1.50 (also http://momo.local). The app shows it too.
  6. Put your phone back on your home Wi-Fi, open that address in Chrome
     and pair once more (new code - each address is paired separately).
  7. Chrome menu (3 dots) > "Add to Home screen" = Momo icon on your phone.

  Later: Momo menu > Phone & Wi-Fi > App address shows the address any time.
  Same menu: Pair a phone, Wi-Fi setup (to change networks), Unpair all.

  How your data is kept safe
   - Only someone who can SEE Momo's screen can pair a phone (6-digit code,
     valid 2 minutes). 5 wrong codes lock pairing for 5 minutes.
   - The phone gets a long secret key; Momo keeps only a fingerprint of it.
     Up to 3 phones; remove any of them in More > Security and backup.
   - Your Wi-Fi password stays inside Momo: it is never shown in the app,
     never sent back to a phone, never printed, and not in backups.
   - Momo is reachable only on your home Wi-Fi, not from the internet.
   - Backup (More > Download backup) saves settings, alarms, reminders and scores
     to your phone as a file; Restore loads it back.

  Your own reminders (tab Reminders > My reminders)
   - "Add a reminder", type what to remember (up to 31 letters), pick the
     time and how it repeats: Once (pick the date), Every day, or Some days.
   - Save my reminders. "Test on Momo" shows it right away.
   - Momo shows your words and rings until you tap it. Hold = again in
     5 minutes. It rings even in quiet hours and at night.
   - A "Once" reminder switches itself off after it rings. Up to 10.
   - The home page shows the next one.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2 - CHRONOS (Bluetooth)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Play Store > install "Chronos" (by fbiego). Open it and allow what it
     asks for: Nearby devices/Bluetooth, Notification access, Phone (calls),
     Location (weather).
  2. Add a watch > choose "Momo" > connect. Momo makes a happy sound.
  3. In Chronos, turn on what you want (names can differ a little between
     Chronos versions):
       Notifications  pick apps (WhatsApp, SMS ...) -> shown on Momo
       Calls          Momo shows who calls (quiet in quiet hours)
       Music          play music on the phone -> Momo shows the song;
                      on Momo's music screen: tap = play/pause,
                      tilt right = next, tilt left = previous
       Weather        Momo shows it as an animated full-screen scene.
                      Momo app > More > Weather: "Weather from" Auto uses
                      the phone first and the internet as a backup;
                      "Phone" uses only Chronos (no Wi-Fi needed);
                      "Get the weather every" sets how often (10 min-3 h)
       Alarms         alarms set in Chronos ring on Momo too
       Water reminder / Sedentary reminder (interval + start/end time)
                      -> Momo's "every X minutes" water / stretch reminders
       Do not disturb (quiet hours) -> Momo's quiet hours
       Sleep time     -> when Momo sleeps and wakes up
       Find watch     -> Momo calls out "I'm here!"
     Chronos also sets Momo's clock when it connects.

  Exact-time reminders (for example water at 10:00, 12:00, 15:30) are set in
  the Momo app (tab Reminders). Settings from Chronos and from the Momo app
  both work; the last one you change wins.
